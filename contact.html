FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response after inquiry?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. WhatsApp messages are typically answered within 2 hours during business days.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product and supplier. For most products, we can accommodate orders starting from 100 kg to 1 MT. Contact us for specific requirements.

Do you provide samples before bulk orders?

Yes! Most suppliers can provide samples at cost price + shipping. Sample delivery typically takes 5–10 business days by air courier.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We support T/T (Bank Transfer), Letter of Credit (LC), and secure escrow arrangements. Payment terms are negotiated per order.
