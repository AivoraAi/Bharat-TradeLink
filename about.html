About Bharat TradeLink

Building bridges between India's finest suppliers and the world's growing markets

Our Story

From India, To the World

Founded in 2018 in Mumbai, Bharat TradeLink was born from a simple belief: India's manufacturers deserve direct access to global buyers — without layers of middlemen, opaque pricing, or paperwork nightmares.

We started as a small team of trade consultants and logistics specialists, and today we facilitate over ₹100 crore in annual exports across metals, spices, chemicals, and textiles to buyers in 50+ countries.

Our mission is to be the most trusted, transparent, and efficient export facilitation platform connecting Indian suppliers with international buyers — driven by technology, powered by relationships.
